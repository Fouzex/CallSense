# CallSense — Team Recording Protocol

## 1. Purpose
This document describes how the CallSense team recorded and documented simulated customer-service conversations for the project's speech-processing and evaluation tasks.

## 2. Recording Setup
- **Dataset size:** 60 recorded conversations
- **Device:** iPhone
- **Application:** Apple Voice Memos
- **Audio format:** M4A
- **Location:** A quiet room at the team members' workplace, without noticeable background disturbance
- **Participants:** More than three team members, with customer and agent roles changing across recordings
- **Duration:** Varied across recordings; some were shorter than one minute and others were longer

## 3. Scripts and Scenario Coverage
A team member prepared an Excel spreadsheet containing predefined customer-service scripts. The recordings cover Modern Standard Arabic (MSA), Saudi Arabic dialect, and Arabic–English code-switching. The scenarios include both escalation and non-escalation cases.

The Excel spreadsheet records the language and escalation category for each call and links each audio filename to its corresponding script.

## 4. Recording Procedure
1. Select a customer-service script from the Excel spreadsheet.
2. Assign the customer and agent roles to the participating team members.
3. Record the scripted conversation using Apple Voice Memos on an iPhone.
4. Review the recording by listening to it and checking speech clarity.
5. Organize the M4A recordings using sequential filenames such as `call_001.m4a`.

## 5. Reference Transcripts
The team listened to the recordings and checked the scripts against the spoken conversations. Reference transcripts were corrected where needed to match the words actually spoken, rather than relying on the original scripts unchanged. These verified transcripts were used as ground truth for word error rate (WER) evaluation.

## 6. Storage and Participant Consent
The recordings are kept in shared project storage. All conversations were scripted simulations rather than actual customer calls. Participants agreed to have their voices recorded and used for the project.
